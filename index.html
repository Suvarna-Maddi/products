Acemain

An effective solution for the control of chewing and sucking insects

Acephate 75% SP is an effective insecticide from Adama, widely used on major crops such as rice and cotton. It works by controlling a broad range of chewing and sucking insects. The target pests include jassids, bollworms, aphids, stem borer, leaf folder, plant hoppers, and green leaf hopper. It is available in convenient pack sizes of 100g, 250g, 500g, and 1kg.
